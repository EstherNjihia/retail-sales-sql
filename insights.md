# Duka Smart Electronics | Business Insights

This analysis covers January 2025 through September 2026. Revenue and customer spending include completed orders after discounts.

## 1. Mobile Phones led in gross profit, while Accessories had the highest margin

The Mobile Phones category recorded the highest revenue (KSh 6,942,060.65) and gross profit (KSh 728,760.65). Accessories generated gross profit of KSh 456,746.10 at a margin of 42.39%, compared with 10.50% for Mobile Phones.  Overall, Mobile Phones contribute the most gross profit, while Accessories earn more gross profit per shilling of revenue.
Try selling compatible accessories at the phone checkout. Before you expand the offer, track how often customers are adding an accessory and see if your gross profit per order increases.

## 2. Nairobi generated about 40% of completed revenue 

Nairobi’s customers accounted for KSh 9,581,980.35, representing 39.67% of the completed revenue. Mombasa came second with KSh 3,161,889.60. Nairobi produced just over three times the revenue of Mombasa.
A slowdown in Nairobi could noticeably impact total revenue, as customers in a single city make up a large portion of sales.
Monitor sales and stock levels in Nairobi on a monthly basis. Test a small campaign in Mombasa and compare the gross profit generated by the campaign against the campaign cost before increasing the budget.

## 3. VIP customers spent the most per registered customer 

The average spend was KSh 253,251.88 for VIP, KSh 170,654.06 for Corporate and KSh 141,123.71 for Retail customers. These averages include registered customers who have not completed any purchases.
VIP customers had the highest revenue per registered customer, making them a good group to test a retention offer.
Give a select group of VIP customers the chance to get early access to new products. Compare repeat purchases and gross profit to a similar group that didn’t get early access.

## 4. Forty-seven customers went more than 90 days without a completed order

As of the last order date in the data, September 30, 2026, 47 of the 132 customers who had completed an order had gone more than 90 days since their last completed purchase. That’s 35.61% of past buyers. The longest gap was 625 days.
More than a third of past buyers are eligible for the inactivity threshold, giving the business a defined group for a win-back campaign.
Begin with inactive customers who have spent the most historically. Send a reminder based on past purchases and track how many complete another order within 30 days.
